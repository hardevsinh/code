Every year, a handful of design conferences stand out from the noise. Here are the ones worth blocking off your calendar for.

## Config

A yearly gathering for product designers, run by Figma. Expect deep dives into design systems, prototyping workflows, and AI-assisted design tools.

## An Event Apart

A long-running web design and UX conference. Smaller, more intimate, and heavy on practical takeaways you can apply the next day.

## Smashing Conference

Run by Smashing Magazine, this one leans into front-end + design crossover topics: performance, accessibility, and CSS techniques.

## Why it's worth going

- Real-world case studies, not just theory
- Direct access to people building the tools you use daily
- A chance to step outside your own bubble and see how other teams work

If travel isn't an option, most of these post recordings online within a few weeks — worth checking their sites even after the fact.
