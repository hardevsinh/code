Picking a typeface is easy. Picking one that actually holds up across a whole product is harder. Here's a short, practical list.

## For UI work

**Inter** and **Poppins** (already used in this portfolio theme) are safe, highly legible choices at small sizes — great for buttons, labels, and body copy.

## For headings and personality

**Fraunces** or **Playfair Display** add character to hero sections without hurting readability, since they're only used at large sizes.

## For code and technical content

**JetBrains Mono** and **Fira Code** are built for readability at small sizes and include programming ligatures.

## A simple rule of thumb

1. Pick one font for UI text
2. Pick one (optional) display font for headings
3. Stop there — more than two families usually looks messy, not sophisticated
